Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our service

How long does a transfer take?

Transfer times vary depending on the destination type. Bank transfers typically take 1-3 business days, cryptocurrency transfers are usually completed within 24 hours, and gift card deliveries are processed within 1-2 business days.

Is my identity protected?

Yes, we offer complete anonymity if desired. We do not store personal information beyond what is necessary to complete your transfer. All communications are encrypted and secure.

What is the minimum transfer amount?

We do not have a strict minimum transfer amount, but please note that our 20% commission applies to all transactions. For very small amounts, the commission may make the transfer less economical.

Which cryptocurrencies do you support?

We support all major cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Litecoin (LTC), and many others. Please specify your preferred cryptocurrency when contacting us.

How do I track my transfer?

Once you initiate a transfer, we provide you with a unique reference number. You can use this to check the status of your transfer by contacting us via email or Telegram.

What happens if there's an issue with my transfer?

Our support team is available to assist you with any issues. Contact us immediately via email or Telegram, and we'll work to resolve the problem as quickly as possible.
